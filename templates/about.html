About This Project

Study Tracker was created to help students stay organized, motivated, and consistent with their learning. It allows users to track study sessions, record weekly progress, and reflect on their goals for each course.

This web app is part of Omar Issa’s CS50 Final Project, built using Flask, SQLite, and Jinja. It combines clean design, interactive tracking, and simple database logic — all centered around helping students manage their study life effectively.

“Small daily progress leads to big long-term success.”
